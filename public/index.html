Session scoreboard & rules

Each player receives two private cards. Make the best five-card hand using any of your cards and the five community cards. Blinds are 10/20. Check when no bet is due, call to match, raise to increase, or fold. Chips are virtual. No turn timer. The host deals each new hand. Guests may join between hands.

Hand order: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card.
